Click Start (or press Space) at the exact instant you power the console on. Beeps sound each second for the last five; press A on the long final beep. Retries reuse the same target: dead-battery boots are identical every time.

Calibration offset: 0 ms

Targets assume the Ruby/Sapphire dead-battery boot seed 0x5A0 and one RNG advance per frame at 59.7275 fps. All timing error is absorbed by the calibration loop.
