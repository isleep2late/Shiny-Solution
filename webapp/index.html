Gen 2 shiny DV checker (also: will my Gen 1 mon be shiny in Gen 2?)

The rule (from the Crystal decompilation): Defense, Speed and Special DVs must all be exactly 10, and the Attack DV must be one of 2, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11, 14, 15. That is 1/8192. Gen 1 has no shininess of its own — but the Time Capsule copies DVs unchanged, so a Gen 1 mon with these DVs IS shiny once traded to Gen 2.

Automated shiny hunting for Gen 1/2 needs an emulator: it is part of the Windows desktop app driving mGBA, not this web version.

About

Shiny-Solution manipulates the actual RNG of the classic Pokémon games: every mechanic here was verified against the pret decompilations. This web version covers everything that needs no emulator: searchers, calculators, checkers and calibrated timers for real-hardware manipulation.

The full desktop version additionally drives the mGBA emulator for frame-perfect automatic manipulation (Gen 3) and automated savestate hunting (Gen 1/2):

  • Windows (full: emulator auto-press + everything here)
  • Linux (calculators & timers)
  • macOS (calculators & timers)

GBA timing: 59.7275 fps. NDS timing: 59.8261 fps. All timing error is absorbed by the calibration loops.
